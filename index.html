Introduction

A project enquiry is the start
of a bigger conversation.

A customer may arrive with a question about one material. Understanding the project opens the door to more useful advice, related products and a clearer route forward.

These demos follow the same customer and adviser across three illustrative projects, from a residential extension to commercial earthworks and a council road scheme.

What we’ve learnt

The value sits in the whole journey.

The themes guiding these demonstrations: preserve what the customer has already shared, support the adviser in the moment, and make the outcome easier to act on.

01 / Context

The project matters as much as the product.

Location, access, delivery timing and the wider job make an enquiry useful. That information needs to follow the customer from the website or bot to the adviser.

02 / Guidance

Helpful advice needs to arrive at the right moment.

Relevant knowledge and checklist prompts can support the conversation while Emily listens, checks requirements and explores related materials.

03 / Follow-through

The next step needs a usable record.

A summary and appropriate wrap-up help capture the discussion, outstanding checks and follow-up, so the conversation can move forward.

These themes shape the three scenarios: context, guidance and a useful next step.

The problems we want to solve

Less friction. More useful conversations.

01

Customers repeating the same details

Carry the enquiry and conversation history into the callback or handover, so the adviser can pick up with context.

02

Time spent finding the right guidance

Surface relevant knowledge and prompts while keeping Emily responsible for reviewing the advice and its fit.

03

Wider project needs going unexplored

Use the project context to discuss complementary products and lower-carbon options where relevant.

04

Administration slowing the next step

Support after-call work with a conversation summary and suggested wrap-up codes ready for adviser review.
